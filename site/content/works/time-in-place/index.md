Interactive projection - see Dublin past in the keyhole of your shadow. Street scenes from Dublin's north inner city, including lost market streets, now absorbed within a shopping center, or reshaped in modern times.

Shown at [Local #1](https://a4sounds.org/locale-1/), A4 Sounds, Dublin, Ireland, 2017.
