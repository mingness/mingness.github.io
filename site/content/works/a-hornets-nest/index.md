---
title: "A Hornet's Nest"
description: 2019
date: 2019-03-21
---
A sculpture in printed tissue and glue. Representative of the hate we weave with words when we communicate with those we cannot see. Composed of words from a few live internet hornets nests at the time. Constructed line by line, as hornet's construct their nests layer by layer.

Inspired partly from my own experience of the toxicity spewed on the internet. When people assume they understand, and then throw shade based on a falsehood.

Exhibited at [C A H O O T S : A 4 \ w / S A M P L E](https://sample-studios.com/exhibitions/cahoots-a4-sounds-with-sample-studios/) at St Luke's Crypt, Cork, Ireland, 2019. Cover photograph by Frank Prendergast.
