Hear what you see - the frames of the video are mapped to audio. Here, clips from Oskar Fischinger's An Optical Poem are given a new soundtrack. A project developed while a student of the workshop [Interventions](https://www.schoolofma.org/interventions) hosted by the School of Machines, Making, and Make Believe, Berlin, Germany, 2015.
