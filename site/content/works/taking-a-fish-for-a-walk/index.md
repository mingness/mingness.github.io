'Taking a fish for a walk' is a video exploration of what lies under the surface. Footage taken at the Royal Canal in Dublin using a GoPro in a homemade floating rig; the name of the piece comes from a question from an onlooker. The video is a collaboration between Claudine Chen and Daniel Brennan, shown at [Locale #3](https://a4sounds.org/locale-3/), A4 Sounds, 2019.
