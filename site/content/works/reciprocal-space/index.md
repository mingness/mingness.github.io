Reciprocal Space consists of a three-dimensional, large-scale sculpture made from plant-milk cartons. The forms reference bodies, but in an alternate reality of smooth mathematical surfaces. The two forms interact, but in unknown abstract ways.

Reciprocal space is a term for a “mathematical map” used to intuit the electronic or electromagnetic behavior of materials, particularly crystals. While real space describes where atoms are physically located, reciprocal space describes patterns or symmetries of those atoms. The word “reciprocal” also recalls reciprocation in interaction.

This year I found myself drawn to using "waste" materials: food packaging, shredded paper, wood ash, dried plants, and hair. In this way, I've transformed for myself a trait that isn't necessarily valued or helpful in other fields (it's basically a scarcity mindset) into characteristic of my artistic practice to treasure. And I also like that I'm taking something otherwise discarded and making it valuable again.

This is the final project of year two of the Higher Diploma program in [NCAD](https://www.ncad.ie/). [See](https://2026.ncad.works/courses/higher-diploma-in-art) the works of all the artists in my class.
